Udemy Learning Streak · v1.0.0

Daily goal

Minutes of lecture content you need to actually watch each day to keep your streak alive.

Changing the goal applies to today (if not completed yet) and future days. Past days keep their original goal.

Tracking

Your goal counts lecture content consumed: how far the video really advanced while it was playing. 5 real minutes at 2× = 10 minutes of content. Actual watch time is tracked separately. Dragging the progress bar (seeking) never counts as watching.

LearnStreak tracks genuine video playback even when you switch tabs or work in another application. Paused, stopped, ended, frozen, locked, or sleeping sessions are not counted.

Learning Library

YouTube counts only for videos you add here. Any other YouTube video — music, entertainment, anything you didn't add — is never counted.

Single videos only. Playlists aren't supported yet — a link with &list=… adds just that one video.

    Notifications

    Quiet, at most one celebration and one reminder per day.

    Data

    Back up your streak or move it to another browser.

    Your learning data is stored locally in your browser. This extension does not send your Udemy activity to an external server. No analytics, no tracking, no accounts. It only measures time — it never changes your Udemy course progress.

    Developer tools

    Simulation only — enabled via DEBUG_TOOLS in src/config/config.js.
